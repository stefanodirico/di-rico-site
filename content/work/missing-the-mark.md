## Why it mattered

Exams shape how young people see themselves, and the pressure starts early. The way we measure young people's success affects their mental health.

## What I did

I helped co-design YoungMinds' Missing the Mark campaign on exam reform, and shared my own experience of sitting exams. The campaign was featured across Sky News channels.

> Let's reduce pressure on young people.
