At 16 I had political opinions. At 17 I was involved in national campaigns for better mental health support. Decisions about young people are better when young people have a say in them.

## What I did

I joined My Life My Say's Squad Leadership Programme, where my cohort elected me Class President. I campaigned for votes at 16, and for voter registration through Give An X.
