## Why it mattered

Thousands of young people were seeking mental health support and being told to wait. Early support hubs are places where a young person can get help without an appointment or a referral, but whether you had one nearby depended on where you lived.

## What I did

With three other YoungMinds Activists, I co-wrote an open letter to the Chancellor asking for a national roll-out of early support hubs, as part of the Fund the Hubs campaign. More than 15,000 people added their names.

On 28 February 2024, ahead of the Spring Budget, we took the letter to 10 Downing Street and handed it in, joined by Dr Alex George.

> 15,000+ signatures, delivered by hand.

## What changed

The hand-in put young people's own words in front of the people writing the Budget, and it was one moment in a longer campaign. Early intervention has stayed at the centre of my work ever since.
