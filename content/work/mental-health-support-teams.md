## Why it mattered

Getting a Mental Health Support Team into every school in the country was a key ask of YoungMinds' End The Wait campaign. The teams offer early help in schools and colleges, before problems become a crisis.

## What I did

I brought my own experience of mental ill health to the people who could change things: politicians, educators and members of the public. That meant speeches, social media campaigns, lobbying MPs at parliamentary events and a lot of one-to-one conversations.

## What changed

The 2025 Spending Review confirmed that Mental Health Support Teams will expand to cover 100% of schools in England by 2029/30. That commitment came from years of work by many young campaigners and organisations, and I am proud to have been one of the voices.

> Every school in England, by 2029/30.
